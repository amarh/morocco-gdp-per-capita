# Morocco's GDP per capita since independence (1956–2022)

An interactive site showing how Morocco's income per person has changed since independence, compared with 11 emerging economies that started from a similar level (Algeria, Tunisia, Egypt, Jordan, Türkiye, South Korea, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines, Vietnam). Spain, Portugal and the United States are included for reference.

**Live site:** https://amarh.github.io/morocco-gdp-per-capita/

## Key findings

- Real GDP per capita rose from $2,313 (1956) to $8,400 (2022), in 2011 international dollars at purchasing-power parity. That is a 3.6-fold increase, or 1.97% a year.
- Morocco ranks 11th of 12 peers for growth. In income it fell from 5th place in 1956 to 10th in 2022.
- Its income stayed at 13–14% of the US level throughout, so it did not catch up.
- The 2000s were its strongest decade (3.8% a year).

## Contents

- `index.html`: the site (static, no build step)
- `data.js`: data and derived statistics used by the charts
- `d3.v7.min.js`: D3 v7 (ISC licence), stored here so the site needs no outside servers
- `downloads/`: full PDF report and CSV data
